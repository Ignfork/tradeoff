What TradeOff is

TradeOff is a place to sell a bag of any Solana coin straight to a buyer, or buy one straight from a holder. One side posts an offer. When the other side takes it, the coins and the SOL swap in one transaction: both move, or nothing does.

No pool is involved, so the coin's price on the charts doesn't move. A big holder can exit without dragging the price down on everyone who's still holding.

Status

The on-chain desk goes live with $TRADEOFF. Markets, Positions and the pool calculators work now. Until launch, the order slip saves your offers as drafts so they're ready to post.

Why not the pool

A pool prices each trade on a curve, so a sale that's large next to the pool fills worse the bigger it gets, and leaves the price lower for everyone. We model a pool as constant product with half its liquidity on each side. Selling D dollars into a side holding R gets an average of R / (R + D) of the market price, and leaves the price at the square of that.

Those are two different costs. "Seller gets" is what the seller loses to slippage. "Chart moves" is what every other holder sees. Real liquidity is spread across several pools and curves, so treat both as estimates. The order slip shows the same comparison for your exact size, and the dump calculator lets you try any pool.

Offers, buy offers, private offers

An offer (selling)

You list coins you hold for a price in SOL. The page checks your balance first. Buyers can take the whole bag, or pieces if you turn on Split the bag.

A buy offer

You name a coin, an amount and a price. Anyone holding that coin can sell into it. Your SOL is wrapped and stays in your wallet until they do.

A private offer

Either of the above, locked to one wallet. Only that wallet can take it, and it stays off the public board. Send them the offer's share link.

Offers stay open for an hour, a day, three days or seven days, and you can cancel any time before then. A split offer always takes everything that's left, or leaves at least one smallest split behind, so nobody gets stuck with a crumb.

Pricing an offer

You set a price per coin. The slip shows the live market price next to it and how far yours sits from it, with quick buttons for market, −2%, −5% and −10%. If your price is more than 30% off the market, the slip warns you before you post.

A good deal beats the pool for both sides. A seller pricing a little under market still gets more than dumping would pay, and a buyer still pays less than buying up the pool would cost. The "Against the pool" panel shows both numbers for your exact size, and tells you by how much your price beats the pool.

What you approve

Posting an offer is one transaction. Your wallet shows what it does before you sign. For selling 40,000,000 of a coin, it looks like this:

TradeOff: post an offer Program published at launch Approve 40,000,000 COIN from your COIN account Delegate TradeOff's program address Price 0.00007282 SOL per coin, 2,912.8 SOL total Takers anyone · whole bag only Ends in 1 day Deposit ~0.0018 SOL, refunded when the offer closes

The approval is the key part. It lets one TradeOff program address move up to that many of your coins, and the program only does that inside a trade that pays you your price in the same transaction. The approval always equals the total of your open offers on that coin. Cancel and it shrinks or disappears. You can also revoke it from your wallet any time.

Where funds sit

  • Sellers' coins stay in the seller's own wallet until the moment of the trade.
  • Buyers' SOL for a buy offer is wrapped into wSOL and stays in the buyer's own wallet until a holder sells into it.
  • The only thing TradeOff stores is the offer record: the terms, what's left, and when it ends. It holds no coins and no SOL.

Who you're trusting

The program

Every rule is enforced by code on Solana: the price, the size, the expiry, who can take a private offer, and that both sides move together. There's no admin key that can touch offers or redirect fees.

Whoever holds the upgrade key

A Solana program can be upgraded or closed by its upgrade key. We'll publish who holds it at launch, and say here if it moves to a multisig or gets locked for good.

Solana and your wallet

Same as any app on Solana.

Risks

Audit status

We'll publish the program's audit status at launch. Until a program has an outside audit, start with amounts you're comfortable with.

Coins carry their own risk

Some coins let their creator freeze wallets. TradeOff marks those Freezable. Coins with transfer fees, transfer hooks or a permanent delegate are refused outright. The slip checks this the moment you pick a coin.

Your price doesn't follow the market

An open offer sells at the price you set even if the market moves. Use shorter expiries, or cancel and repost.

One approval per coin account

A coin account can only have one approved app at a time. Posting on TradeOff replaces another app's approval on that coin.

Pool figures are estimates

The "against the pool" numbers come from total pool liquidity. Real pools can be thinner or deeper.

FAQ

Does TradeOff hold my coins or SOL?

No. Coins stay in the seller's wallet and SOL stays wrapped in the buyer's wallet until the moment of the trade. TradeOff only stores the offer record.

Can TradeOff take my coins?

The approval only lets the program move your coins inside a trade that pays you your price in the same transaction, and never more than your open offers. You can revoke it any time.

What if the price changes while I'm buying?

You pay the price on the offer, not the market price. If the seller cancels first, the transaction fails and nothing moves.

Which coins work?

Any standard Solana coin. Coins with transfer fees, transfer hooks or a permanent delegate are refused. Freezable coins work but are marked.

Why not just sell on the pool?

Because a big sale fills worse the bigger it is, and drops the chart for every holder. On TradeOff the price is whatever you and the buyer agree, and the chart doesn't move.

Where are my draft offers stored?

In this browser, until the on-chain desk is live. Nothing is sent anywhere.
